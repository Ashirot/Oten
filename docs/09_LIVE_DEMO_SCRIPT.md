# LIVE DEMONSTRATION & DEFENSE SCRIPT
## Pastil Rice Davao — Database Systems 1

**Video Duration:** ~5–8 minutes  
**Format:** Step-by-step narration script for presenting the live system to the instructor.

---

### Phase 1: Introduction & System Overview (1 Minute)
- **Visual:** Open browser at `http://127.0.0.1:8000`. Show the Pastil Rice Davao storefront.
- **Narration:**
  > "Good day, Professor. Today we present our Database Systems 1 Final Project: 'Pastil Rice Davao', a database-driven ordering, inventory, and sales management system. We implemented the backend using Laravel 11 with a REST API architecture, the frontend using ReactJS with Tailwind CSS, running on a MySQL 8.0 InnoDB database. We selected Topic 2: Inventory and Sales Management System."

---

### Phase 2: Customer Ordering Flow & Automated Trigger Deduction (2 Minutes)
- **Visual:**
  1. Click on **Beef Pastil** or **Combo Box**. Open the quantity picker modal, select 2 units, and add to cart.
  2. Open the Cart Drawer. Note the subtotal and the free delivery discount calculation.
  3. Click **Proceed to Checkout**. Fill in customer details: Name, Mobile, and Davao Address (e.g., Matina, Davao City). Point out the interactive Leaflet GPS Map of Davao City.
  4. Select **Cash on Delivery (COD)** and click **Confirm & Place Order**.
- **Narration:**
  > "When the customer places an order, Laravel initiates an ACID database transaction. It issues a `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` pessimistic lock on the product table to verify that stock is available. When the order items are inserted, our MySQL trigger `trg_after_order_item_insert` immediately fires at the database engine level. It decrements the product's inventory and automatically records an audit entry in `inventory_logs`."
- **Visual:**
  Show the **Live Order Tracker Modal** tracking progress (`Cooking & Packing` &rarr; `Out for Delivery` &rarr; `Delivered`), and submit a 5-star review.

---

### Phase 3: Administrative Dashboard & Stored Procedure Execution (2 Minutes)
- **Visual:**
  1. Switch to the **Management & DB Dashboard** tab.
  2. Click **Products & Inventory**. Show the products table, pointing out the UDF stock status badge (`IN_STOCK`, `LOW_STOCK`).
  3. Click the green **Package Restock** button on Tuna Pastil. Enter 50 units and click **Execute sp_RestockProduct**.
  4. Click **Audit Logs** to show the live audit trail generated by the trigger and stored procedure.
- **Narration:**
  > "Here in the Management Dashboard, we can observe our MySQL Stored Procedure `sp_RestockProduct` in action. When we restock, the stored procedure updates the inventory atomically and inserts the audit record. If we view the Audit Logs, we see both automated sales deductions from our trigger and restock additions from our stored procedure."

---

### Phase 4: Database Diagnostic Laboratory (2 Minutes)
- **Visual:**
  1. Click the **Database Laboratory (DB 1)** tab.
  2. In **Section D (Query Optimization)**, switch between Example 1 (Composite Indexing) and Example 2 (Correlated Subquery Elimination). Show the before and after `EXPLAIN` execution plans.
  3. In **Section E (Transactions)**, click **Run COMMIT Demo** to show an atomic success, then click **Run ROLLBACK Demo**. Show the trigger error message rejecting negative stock and the subsequent rollback.
  4. Click **Section B (Data Dictionary)** and cycle through tables to show real-time schemas from MySQL `INFORMATION_SCHEMA`.
- **Narration:**
  > "To verify our academic deliverables, we built this live Database Laboratory. Under Query Optimization, we demonstrate how our composite index `(order_status, created_at)` transforms a full table scan into an index range scan. Under Transactions, we demonstrate how our trigger `trg_prevent_negative_stock` throws SQLSTATE 45000 if an illegal update occurs, triggering an immediate ROLLBACK to preserve complete database consistency."

---

### Phase 5: Business Intelligence Reports & Conclusion (1 Minute)
- **Visual:**
  1. Click **Business Reports**.
  2. Show Report 1 (Sales Analytics from `vw_sales_summary`), Report 2 (Low Stock Reorder Alerts from `vw_low_stock_alerts`), Report 3 (Top Products from `vw_product_performance`), and Report 4 (Customer Lifetime Value using `fn_CalculateCustomerTotalSpent`).
- **Narration:**
  > "Finally, our reporting suite aggregates data using MySQL views and functions, empowering managers to forecast demand, prevent stockouts, and analyze customer spending. All 13 final project deliverables—including DDL, DML, triggers, procedures, functions, normalization up to 3NF, and test results—are complete and fully operational. Thank you!"
